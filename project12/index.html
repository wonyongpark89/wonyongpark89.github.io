Remembering Forward: Co-designing a Youth-Informed Grenfell Partnership

Project periodFrom November 2026
PartnersUniversity of Oxford, London Museum, Grenfell Tower Memorial Commission and The Grenfell Foundation
StatusCurrent

Summary

What should future generations learn and remember about Grenfell, and how can a museum support that work? Ahead of the tenth anniversary of the Grenfell Tower fire in June 2027, this partnership brings young adults affected by Grenfell together with museum staff, researchers and community partners to explore those questions.

The project builds on the Grenfell Curriculum’s community research. It will use London Museum’s High Rise display at Smithfield as a starting point for discussing whose accounts shape public memory and how museums can tell difficult histories with care.

Activities

Two co-design workshops will bring together six young adults aged 18–25. Using museum objects and community accounts shared with permission, participants will consider which stories and materials they want future generations to inherit. They will develop a draft set of Youth Principles for Remembering Grenfell and review it with partners in a joint session.

Participants will decide which of their contributions to include and how to credit them. The workshops will include support for participants’ access and wellbeing.

Expected outputs

The Remembering Forward Partnership Brief will bring together the draft Youth Principles, a written response from London Museum and a plan for future learning and remembrance activities. The principles will be a starting point for wider community discussion.

Research team
