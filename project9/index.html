Research Project

STEM Participation and Aspiration in Ultra-Low-Fertility South Korea

Home› Projects› STEM Participation and Aspiration in Ultra-Low-Fertility South Korea
Project period2025 – 2027
FunderThe Academy of Korean Studies
PartnersJeju National University
StatusCurrent

Summary

This project examines STEM education in small schools in Jeju, South Korea, in the context of declining school-age populations. It explores how changes in school communities and provision shape young people’s opportunities to participate in STEM and their aspirations for further study and future careers.

Drawing on ethnographic research, the study considers how small schools respond to demographic change and what their experiences can contribute to education policy and practice.

Research Questions

  • How is the demographic decline shifting STEM education in small schools in South Korea?
  • How does such a change impact students' opportunities to participate in STEM and pursue STEM careers?

Team

  • Wonyong Park, University of Southampton (Principal Investigator)
  • Yeonjoo Ko, Jeju National University (Co-Investigator)
  • Seongeon Hong, Kyungmoon High School (Research Assistant)

Project status and publications

The project is ongoing and has received a one-year extension. No publications have yet been produced.
